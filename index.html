1. Privacy Philosophy

WishPing is built as a local-first mobile application. This means our core operating guidelines prioritize your absolute data sovereignty:

  • No External Servers: We do not own, operate, or maintain any backend servers that collect, store, or process your personal event data, logs, or API credentials.
  • On-Device Storage: All of your personal data, including contact names, birthdays, anniversaries, categories, templates, and API keys, remains encrypted/stored strictly on your device.
  • Direct Client-Side Integration: Any third-party APIs you configure are contacted directly from your device. Your tokens and request payloads never pass through any middleman.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect or harvest any information. However, to operate, the Application stores and processes the following parameters directly on your device:

A. Local Event & Contact Data

To help you track and remember special occasions, you can input and manage details including:

  • Contact Profiles: Recipient names, custom relationships, and custom templates.
  • Events: Exact calendar dates (birthdays, anniversaries, other special events) and custom notification times.
  • Storage Platform: Saved locally using the device's native secure local storage interface (AsyncStorage).

B. System Permissions

  • Local Notifications: The app requests permission to schedule system alerts. All calculations and triggers for upcoming events are scheduled locally on your device via expo-notifications. No push servers are involved.

3. Google OAuth & Gmail Integration

If you choose to link your Google Account to automatically send greeting cards or emails directly from WishPing, the integration functions under strict parameters:

Secure Local Tokens

We use the official Google OAuth client interface. The Application requests limited API access. Your tokens are managed locally:

  • Tokens: oauthAccessToken and oauthRefreshToken are stored on your device only.
  • Profile Metadata: Profile picture, display name, and email address are cached locally to customize your dashboard layout.

Direct Transmission

When you trigger an email wish, the Application sends the request directly to the Gmail REST API (https://gmail.googleapis.com) from your phone. The email content, metadata, and body are never transmitted to any servers owned by WishPing or other third parties.

4. Google Gemini AI Integration

WishPing integrates with the Google Gemini API to generate customized, high-quality, and contextual greetings. Here is how your data is handled:

Your Own API Key

The Application does not bundle a centralized AI key. Instead, you supply your personal Google Gemini API Key in Settings. This key is saved locally in your phone's storage, ensuring no other entities can access it.

Data Sent to Gemini API

To generate the wish content, the Application sends a tailored prompt directly to Google's Gemini API endpoints (https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com). The payload includes:

  • The recipient's name
  • Your relationship to them (e.g., friend, sibling)
  • The type of event (e.g., birthday, anniversary)
  • The requested tone (e.g., humorous, formal, heartwarming)
  • Your custom notes and event preferences

These requests are subject to Google's official developer API terms. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle developer API payloads.

5. Data Security

Since WishPing uses a client-only architecture, the security of your stored data depends on your physical device and operating system environment.

Recommendation: We strongly advise configuring device-level security parameters (e.g., biometric login, complex passcodes, and disk encryption) to ensure your local data is protected from unauthorized physical access.

6. Retention & Data Erasure

You have full control over your data. We store your preferences and events indefinitely on your device, but you can wipe them instantly at any time:

Immediate Wiping

You can execute a hard reset of your workspace inside the Application:

  1. Go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Danger Zone.
  3. Select Clear All App Data.

All database records, settings, and Google authentication cache keys will be permanently deleted from the device's storage. Deleting or uninstalling the Application also clears the local sandbox data entirely.
